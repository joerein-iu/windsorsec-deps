    How it works

    1. 1

      Discover

      Your public DNS shows who runs your email, sign-in, DNS and website, plus which SaaS tools have verified your domain. It's public information, read the same way any mail server reads it.

    2. 2

      Weigh

      For each dependency: what stops if it's down, whether you have a fallback, and whether more than one person can get into it.

    3. 3

      Rank

      Every gap gets a risk score and a rough cost. Fixes are ranked by risk reduced per dollar, so the free and cheap wins come first.

    Why this exists

    In 2026 CISA discontinued several free assessments, including its External Dependencies Management assessment. Small businesses rarely had access to those reviews anyway, and one bad vendor update or cloud outage can stop thousands of businesses that did nothing wrong. This is a free, open replacement for the part that matters most to small teams: knowing what you depend on and what to do about it. Methodology and scoring are in the repo.

    How it works

    01 · Discover

    Read your public DNS

    Your DNS records show who runs your email, your sign-in, your DNS and your website, plus which SaaS tools have verified your domain. It's public information, read the same way any mail server reads it.

    02 · Weigh

    Ask what an outage costs

    For each dependency: what stops if it's down, whether you have a fallback, and whether more than one person can get into it.

    03 · Rank

    Spend where it counts

    Every gap gets a risk score and a rough cost. Fixes are ranked by risk reduced per dollar, so the free and cheap wins come first.

    Why this exists

    In 2026 CISA discontinued several free assessments, including its External Dependencies Management assessment. Small businesses rarely had access to those reviews anyway. Meanwhile one bad vendor update or cloud outage can stop thousands of businesses that did nothing wrong. This is a free, open replacement for the part that matters most to small teams: knowing what you depend on and what to do about it. The methodology and scoring are in the repo.
